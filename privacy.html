Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This page explains what information the Noise Level Studio website uses and why. We keep it simple: we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details on this website.

What we don't collect

We don't use accounts, cookies, ads or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share any information about you.

Bubbles popped count

The number of bubbles you pop is saved in your own browser, only while the tab is open. It never gets sent to us, and it is cleared when you refresh the page or close the tab.

Services we use

To load the website, your browser connects to a few outside services. Like any website, they can see basic details such as your IP address and browser type.

  • Google Fonts - for the fonts on this website
  • jsDelivr - for Three.js, which draws the bubbles and fish

Other websites

Our links to Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn take you to other websites. They have their own privacy policies, so please check those too.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact us

If you have any questions about your privacy, you can message us on any of our social media accounts linked at the bottom of this page.
